# Feather Storm

**Feather Storm — Naya Tempest Hawk Swarm**

Commander **Jetmir, Nexus of Revels** · Naya ({R}{G}{W}) · bracket 2 (0 Game Changers) · 100 cards

## The plan

Tempest Hawk swarm. 19 copies of Tempest Hawk (the card allows any number), each one that connects tutors another. Cost reducers (Urza's Incubator, Semblance Anvil, Oketra's Monument, Herald's Horn, Dazzling Theater's convoke) drop Hawks toward {W} so several deploy per turn, while Oketra's Monument, Combat Calligrapher, Murmuration, Hero of Bladehold, Assemble the Legion and Felidar Retreat add bodies, doubled by Anointed Procession and Parallel Lives. At nine creatures Jetmir grants +3/+0, vigilance, trample and double strike for the alpha strike. Voice of Victory stops opponents casting during that turn, Heroic Intervention answers a wrath, and Winds of Abandon clears the table one-sidedly.
